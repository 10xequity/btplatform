Membership levels

A level is what a member gets. A plan (under Money) is how they pay for it. Keeping them separate means you can comp a coach to All-Access without pretending they bought a subscription, and a gated schedule can ask "is this member at least Silver?" and get a real answer. Higher rank outranks lower.
